## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-27

### Added

- Source-backed event schema and validation for categories, statuses, dates, unique IDs and factual signatures, and credential-free HTTPS sources.
- Three verified 2026 Federal Open Market Committee event records.
- Responsive monthly calendar with lunar context, date-range lookup, and accessible event details.
- Canonical, Open Graph, schema.org, sitemap, robots, and AI-discovery metadata.
- Automated validation and GitHub Pages deployment workflows.
- Verified 1600 x 900 product preview asset.
- Equivalent English and Chinese project documentation, contribution policy, governance, security reporting, and citation metadata.
